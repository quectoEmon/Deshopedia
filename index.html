      About Us

      Who we are and why we built Smart City

      Smart City Bangladesh is a district information website built by a team of four. Pick any of the 64 districts and find its conditions, places to visit, institutions, helplines and farming advice on one page.

      Our purpose

      Local facts are spread over many sites and apps. We want a visitor, a student, a farmer or a family in a hurry to find what they need about a district in one place.

      Who it helps

      • Travellers planning a trip and its travel time
      • Students looking for universities and colleges
      • Farmers who need fertilizer amounts for their land
      • Anyone who needs a helpline number fast

      Where we are today

      All 64 districts are in. Temperature, air quality, traffic and power are example values for now, and we plan to connect real sources. The red zone map stays empty until we have verified crime statistics.

      Our team

      Temperature, air quality, traffic and power are example numbers that show the layout. Helpline numbers, map links and fertilizer rates are reference information; confirm fertilizer amounts with your local Upazila Agriculture Office (DAE).
